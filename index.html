A conversation about learning in the age of AI

AI changed the way we work.
It shouldn't change the way we think.

A short story about how technology shortened the path to answers — and why curiosity, judgement and human thinking matter more than ever.

01 — The way we learned

If something didn't work,
we had a loop.

We Googled it. We read the documentation. We asked the person sitting next to us. We tried something. We broke something else. We undid it. And we tried again.
